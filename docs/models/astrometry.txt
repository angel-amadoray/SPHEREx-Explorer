# Astrometry Data Models

## What They Represent  
These models capture spatial referencing information for astronomical images, enabling precise conversion between sky coordinates and image pixels.

---

## Key Models

### AstrometryWCS  
Represents the World Coordinate System (WCS) parameters of an image, including:

- Reference sky coordinates (crval1, crval2).  
- Reference pixel coordinates (crpix1, crpix2).  
- Coordinate types, e.g. RA and Dec (ctype1, ctype2).  
- Transformation matrix (`cd`) linking pixel offsets to angular sky offsets.  
- A flag indicating if complex distortion (SIP) correction is present.

### AstrometryResponse  
Provides metadata about an astrometric image:

- Identifiers for sector, epoch, and detector used.  
- Filename and image shape (height and width in pixels).  
- The associated WCS information to enable spatial mapping.

---

## Practical Use  
These models are critical for converting user interactions on images (like clicking pixels) into sky positions and vice versa, supporting accurate spatial analyses and visualizations.
