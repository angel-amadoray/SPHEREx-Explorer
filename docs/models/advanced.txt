# Data Models for Advanced Spectral Analysis

## What They Represent  
These data models define structured formats for representing detailed results of spectral and photometric analyses of SPHEREx observations—specifically focused on paired detector data, pixel brightness, and target suggestion.

---

## Key Models Explained

### DetectorSample  
Represents measurements from a single detector for a specific pixel, including its location, flux, signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), and wavelength details.

### PairClassification  
Encodes the classification of spectral features in a paired detector analysis, such as whether the source shows absorption lines, emission, continuum, or if it’s masked or undetermined.

### PairSpectrumResponse  
Aggregates data for a pair of detectors observing the same sky position, including classification, flux ratio, and whether cached data was used.

### BrightPixel & BrightestPixelsResponse  
Describe individual bright pixels in a detector image and provide a response listing the N brightest pixels along with their celestial coordinates.

### SuggestedTarget & SuggestTargetResponse  
Represent a selected target pixel confirmed to have valid flux in both short- and long-wavelength detectors, along with metadata about how many candidate pixels were examined and reasons for selection or failure.

### BrightPixelItem & BrightestValidResponse  
Represent detailed measurements for top bright pixels valid in paired detectors, including ranks, fluxes, SNRs, pixel coordinates, and flux ratios.

### Extended PairClassification (stellar types)  
Classifies objects based on spectral analysis into stars of different temperature classes (hot star, sun-like star, cool star, etc.), including estimated color temperature and confidence levels.

---

## Use in Practice  
These models provide a standardized way to exchange detailed analytical results between backend services, helping astronomers precisely characterize and select interesting targets for further study.
